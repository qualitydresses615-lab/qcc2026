Our Story

A Family Business, Not Just a Store.

Quality Dresses opened its doors in 1990 with one goal — bring honest quality and real style to every family in Bharuch. Three and a half decades later, that promise hasn't changed, even as the store has grown into a 4,500+ sq ft showroom carrying fashion for every member of the family.
